    Financial autonomy status

    On 11 July 2024 the Supreme Court ruled that local government allocations from the Federation Account should be paid directly to the councils. Press analyses report that many states still control those funds and that implementation remains incomplete nationally.

    Ogbomoso North: the council has not yet published whether its FAAC allocation is paid directly to the council account or through the State/Joint Account. This page will state it as soon as the council confirms it.

    A BudgIT analysis, reported by OyoInsight, found that no local government in Oyo State receives less than ₦100 million a month from FAAC. That is a state-wide floor, not the figure for Ogbomoso North.

    Sources: The PUNCH analysis (reposted); OyoInsight on BudgIT; Vanguard, Dec 2025
